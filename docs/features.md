The owner set the 2 GiB GPU budget, under the direction that interactive speed comes before memory, and the 256 MiB boundary bound ([design](design/gpu-preview.md), [decisions](decisions.md#gpu-previews)). Colour, masks, Presence, Detail and supported chains are enabled after M4 tests against their CPU references. Every plan starts from the photograph's source, which the GPU holds once for every surface, charged to the budget and uploaded at most 32 MiB a frame, and every boundary is derived from it on the GPU: the source reduced to the view's size at Fit and below 100%, at a reduced stage the GPU plans itself, a window of it cut at full scale at 100% and above, whose key includes both the visible output region and the spatial input window. Dehaze's atmospheric light is computed every frame on the GPU from the whole content stage at full resolution, at every view, at rest and in motion, a colour drag under Dehaze included; behind Detail or another spatial layer it is computed exactly from that layer's output by the picture at rest's and an export's staged sweeps, the sweep before the light writing that output into a stage texture the light is reduced from; a drag that leaves the light's input unchanged, or changes it through that layer, reads the light kept at rest, and only a colour drag between them computes the stand-in with those layers left out. A sample through such a light reads it as the tile worker's own sweeps of the stack computed it, once a stack. Where the stage texture does not fit, as on a 60 MP RAW behind Detail, a light sweep with no stage texture draws the layers before the light once more, tile by tile, reducing each tile into the light, at rest, in export and for a sample; the reference renderer draws only where no light sweep tile fits, named `light-stage`. The tile worker computes each light once for an export or a read, a window of the source at a time, or from its staged sweeps' stage texture, so a GPU export of a Dehaze stack draws on the GPU too ([design](design/gpu-preview.md#spatial-programs)). The committed stack's job derives the boundary every gesture over it starts from, so a gesture's first tick is drawn on the GPU. A plan runs as a chain of links, each kept in an intermediate and run only when it changes; a masked spatial layer's passes run over its mask alone; and a painted or moved mask's tick evaluates each link only where its change reaches, bit for bit what a whole evaluation draws, so painting over three masks holding Clarity and Texture, Detail beside them on a RAW, draws every tick within a display frame, p95 about 9 ms at Fit against 158 ms on the CPU path ([performance](specs/performance.md#painting-over-masked-spatial-layers)). The chain's links take their scratch planes in turn from the slot's one pool, which the surface, the qualification and the desktop charge as one figure: at three masks the GPU-preview peak is 23 to 44% lower, and 16 masked Presence layers fit the 2 GiB budget at Fit. Over 10 to 16 masked Presence layers at Fit every tick is drawn on the GPU, but at p95 23 to 24 ms, above the 16 ms target. At 100% the window grows with every chained spatial layer, so the paint harness's layout fits eight masks in its own view; a larger stack there is drawn on the GPU from its reduced stage scaled to the view, the softer drag frame, the status bar saying `Softer while dragging`, and is sharp again at rest ([design](design/gpu-preview.md#at-100-and-above)). JPEG geometry uses an 8-bit segment intermediate; RAW geometry uses `rgba32float` so resampling preserves extended linear values. A native RAW geometry regression matches CPU output codes, and the corpus draws every RAW geometry cell within the limits through the `rgba32float` intermediate at Fit and 100%; an in-editor memory measurement of a photo-sized RAW drag remains outstanding. At 100% and above, previews draw the visible region at full scale when the boundary and slot fit their limits beside the source; otherwise the reduced stage scaled to the view. A RAW white-balance drag is drawn on the GPU over the planes the entry developed, the drafted change a leading step, and a geometry drag on a stack of geometry alone from the source ([design](design/gpu-preview.md#raw-white-balance)). A tick the GPU does not draw holds the frame on screen for a reason that passes within a tick or an upload — its first tick waiting for its boundary, a compile, an upload — for at most half a second, and otherwise the reference renderer draws one whole frame of the drafted stack at a time, the newest tick's next (owner, 2026-10-05; [design](design/gpu-preview.md#a-tick-the-gpu-does-not-draw)); a layer the GPU has no program for and a drag past the budget even at its reduced stage take that path. A session the GPU does not draw at all — no adapter, `--no-gpu-render`, a host with only a software adapter not adopted, a lost device — drags on the display-size CPU proxy instead, latest winning, magnified at 100% and above and labelled approximate, and each release draws the reference's frame sharp (owner, 2026-10-06; [design](design/gpu-preview.md#fallback-and-portability)); the `no-gpu-render` scenario checks it at Fit and at 100%. There is no GPU-preview preference: the GPU draws every frame it can, and `--no-gpu-render` is the launch switch for the reference. A gesture the GPU does not draw for a reason that lasts says why in the status bar: a muted phrase after the render slot with a one-sentence tooltip, one phrase for each mapped class of reason and the layer's name for the stack's, from its first tick until the next GPU frame or the end of its settle, nothing for a reason that passes, and `compiling` only after half a second; while the reference renderer draws every frame, the status bar says "Reference renderer" at rest as well (accepted by the owner on 2026-10-07, [design](design/gpu-preview.md#labels-and-overlays-during-motion)); the core `light-stage` refusal remains unmapped by that notice table and needs an explicit check. The `gpu-preview` and `gpu-preview-zoom` scenarios cover editor interactions, cancellation and the picture at rest after each release, and the latter's drags at 50% and 33% are drawn on the GPU from the source reduced to that view; the release gate measures the corpus at 33% and 50% beside Fit.
