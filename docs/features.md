Detail (noise reduction and capture sharpening) has a GPU program, enabled after matching its CPU kernels and units on the M4 and meeting the spatial limits on the corpus's Detail recipes at Fit against the CPU's moving proxy it stands in for, as the owner decided a stack that settles from the exact render is judged; a Detail drag at Fit draws on the GPU from the Detail layer's input, and a drag of a later layer starts its boundary from the restoration-prefix proxy cache ([performance](specs/performance.md#gpu-detail-program)). A plan chains every spatial and restoration layer after its boundary, the colour layers between them, so a Detail drag with Presence in the stack draws on the GPU too, its chained recipes meeting the spatial limits at Fit; a chain whose applies would read more than 11 planes names `spatial-chain` ([performance](specs/performance.md#after-detail-presence)). A gesture at Fit — a slider drag, a mask's shape moved, a brush stroke — draws its ticks on the GPU over the input of the earliest layer it changes, which the preview worker renders once per draft after the first tick's frame and the surface holds (`rgba16float` on a JPEG, `rgba32float` on a RAW), with no preview job per tick; its plan travels in the draft's own answer, its pipelines compile off the interface thread and are warmed when the stack changes, and the geometry tail after the colour (a straightened crop or a perspective warp through its exact matrix, a lens warp through its coordinate grid) and the steps after it draw on the GPU too, the output encoded as the CPU's quantizer encodes it. A Presence drag, and a drag of a layer under Presence, draw on the GPU as well: Presence's compute passes run only when what they read changed, so a Texture or Clarity drag runs at most five a tick, and Dehaze's light is read from the store its committed frame filled, or taken on the GPU and labelled approximate when the drag changes the light's input. A Presence or Detail drag keeps one compiled sequence while a value crosses zero, at Fit and at a zoom wherever its slot of every unit fits the budget, and the committed stack warms it. Each GPU frame is checked against the CPU frame of the same settings in the `gpu-preview` scenario, and the corpus's colour, geometry, mask and Presence recipes meet their limits at Fit ([performance](specs/performance.md#gpu-colour-programs-at-fit)). At 100% and above a drag is drawn the same way over the visible region at full scale, in place of the half-scale motion region: the first tick's region job carries the region's boundary request, later ticks send no job and ask for no region job while the frame holds the view, the frame is drawn alone at its place in the photograph, a pan past the region draws the CPU's frames until the new region's boundary is held, a region whose boundary or slot would pass its bound or the budget keeps the CPU path naming `budget-exceeded`, a Dehaze drag reads its light from the store the exact visible region filled or keeps the CPU path (`region-estimate`), the mask overlay keeps the CPU path at a percentage zoom (`overlay-shown`), the settle dissolves into the view's own frame as at Fit, a change of zoom or a pan cancelling it, and the desktop keeps no copy of a boundary's texels once the surface holds them (`boundary-released` asks for them again), the surface uploading a new boundary 32 MiB a frame (`boundary-uploading` until it is whole), so a 212 MB boundary's arrival holds 2.2 times its bytes where it held 3.1; the `gpu-preview-zoom` scenario drags at 100%, 200% and, panning, 800%, and Presence at 100%: a Texture drag runs 5 compute passes a tick and a Clarity drag none, and a Basic drag under Presence draws on the GPU unless Dehaze's light would come from the region alone (`region-estimate`). Over the corpus at 100% in the largest window the M4's display holds every measured cell meets its limits, every Presence combination on the JPEGs among them, the peak all three fields on the 60 MP JPEG at 567.6 MB of the 640 MiB budget; a RAW Presence stack with Clarity, its `f32` boundary up to 212 MB within the 256 MiB bound on one, draws on the GPU at 430.7 to 668.2 MB, and all three Presence fields and Detail's moderate and noise-reduction slots on the Air 2S pass the budget even in the CPU's shape and keep the CPU path, naming it ([performance](specs/performance.md#gpu-previews-at-100), [design](design/gpu-preview.md#at-100-and-above)). The `gpu_preview` preference (`workspace.set`, `session.state`, `schema.list` and a palette entry, on by default) gates every plan; the status bar reads `GPU preview · N ms` while a GPU frame is shown, N being the interface thread's time to prepare it, since Iced's device has no timestamp queries; and when a gesture settles — its commit, or its settings rendered on the CPU — the CPU's frame replaces the GPU frame through a 150 ms linear-light dissolve, never to older content, cancelled by any input, with redraws only while it runs. While a clipping overlay is shown a GPU drag marks its own clipped pixels, approximate, in place of the CPU frame's overlay; the mask tint stays the coverage worker's. The `gpu-preview` scenario checks each dissolve's identities, the jump at settle within the pointwise limits, idle after it, the preference, the marks and a cancel; the corpus at settle is within its class's limits on every measured cell ([performance](specs/performance.md#gpu-preview-settle)). Qualified natively on the M4 ([performance](specs/performance.md#gpu-previews-qualified-on-the-m4)): over the corpus, the zone plate and a zone plate carrying a camera and lens identity among its sources, every measured cell at Fit and 100% meets its class's limits, a perspective warp over the zone plate (worst block 0.04 at Fit, through its homography in `f32`) and a lens warp over the second (0.23 at 100%, through a grid held within 0.025 output pixels) among them; `editor-latency`, timing each frame on the path that drew it, puts a 30-input drag's p95 at 8.8 to 9.5 ms at Fit at 24 MP and 60 MP, 9.4 at 100%, 8.6 at 200%, 8.6 to 9.5 for Presence drags on the X100VI and 8.5 for a brush stroke's positions, against 10.1 to 47.2 ms with the preview off in the same build; with five exports holding the shared pool a 60 MP drag's p95 is 18.7 ms (211.5 with the preview off), and a 24 MP one's 46.0.
