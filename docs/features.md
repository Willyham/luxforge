| GPU previews | Implemented; RAW geometry qualification in progress | Gestures draw on the GPU during edits and return to the CPU frame on settle; the CPU remains the reference for analysis, sampling and export. The owner set the 2 GiB GPU budget, under the direction that interactive speed comes before memory, and the 256 MiB boundary bound ([design](design/gpu-preview.md), [decisions](decisions.md#gpu-previews)). Colour, masks, Presence, Detail and supported chains are enabled after M4 tests against their CPU references. Every gesture starts from the input of the stack's first content layer, a boundary held resident between gestures, so its first tick is drawn on the GPU; at percentage zoom its cache key includes both the visible output region and the spatial input window. A plan runs as a chain of links, each kept in an intermediate and run only when it changes; a masked spatial layer's passes run over its mask alone; and a painted or moved mask's tick evaluates each link only where its change reaches, bit for bit what a whole evaluation draws, so painting over three masks holding Clarity and Texture, Detail beside them on a RAW, draws every tick within a display frame, p95 about 9 ms at Fit against 158 ms with the preview off ([performance](specs/performance.md#painting-over-masked-spatial-layers)). The chain's links take their scratch planes in turn from the slot's one pool, which the surface, the qualification and the desktop charge as one figure: at three masks the GPU-preview peak is 23 to 44% lower, and 16 masked Presence layers fit the 2 GiB budget at Fit. Over 10 to 16 masked Presence layers at Fit every tick is drawn on the GPU, but at p95 23 to 24 ms, above the 16 ms target. At 100% the window grows with every chained spatial layer, so the paint harness's layout fits eight masks in its own view; a larger stack there is drawn on the CPU, the status bar saying `GPU memory full` ([design](design/gpu-preview.md#bounds)). JPEG geometry uses an 8-bit segment intermediate; RAW geometry uses `rgba32float` so resampling preserves extended linear values. A native RAW geometry regression matches CPU output codes, and the corpus draws every RAW geometry cell within the limits through the `rgba32float` intermediate at Fit and 100%; an in-editor memory measurement of a photo-sized RAW drag remains outstanding. At 100% and above, previews draw the visible region at full scale when the boundary and slot fit their limits; otherwise the CPU path names the fallback. The `gpu_preview` preference gates every plan. A gesture drawn on the CPU path for a reason that lasts says why in the status bar: a muted phrase after the render slot with a one-sentence tooltip, one phrase for each class of reason and the layer's name for the stack's, from its first tick until the next GPU frame or the end of its settle, nothing for a reason that passes or the preference off, and `compiling` only after half a second (recorded defaults the owner can revise, [design](design/gpu-preview.md#labels-and-overlays-during-motion)). The `gpu-preview` and `gpu-preview-zoom` scenarios cover editor interactions and cancellation, and the latter's drag at 50% captures the notice beside the `not-fit` reason it records.
