| Masks | Delivered (phases A and B); phases C and D in progress | Local adjustments as a host concept, authorized 2026-09-23 and phased in the [design](design/masking.md). **Implemented:** the recipe carries its masks and a layer an optional mask reference, in catalog format 6 and recipe format 2, with structural validation and retention of a component kind this build does not know; `render.transform` answers the geometry tail as one affine; the coverage mathematics is frozen by the [mask study](design/mask-study.md) against an independent `f64` reference, and the linear and radial gradients are transcribed from it bit-identically, each with a conservative support rectangle and a feature width; the `mask.*` host command family is declared with the same descriptor, control and generic-validation machinery a module action uses, drafted so one gesture is one entry, with labels that name the component and the mask and a destructive delete that names the layers it removed; a `maskable` effect flag — Basic, the colour mixer and Presence — puts one optional host-owned `mask` field on every action of such a module, with per-target `single_layer` and placement after the global layer in the mask list's order; **a masked colour layer and a masked spatial layer both change a picture**, blending the operation's output against its own input in linear light — inside the colour run before its single clamp, and at the tile write for a neighbourhood operation, leaving halos, tiling, the spatial budget and cancellation untouched — with the bounds rectangle skipping spans and copying tiles it cannot reach, on the byte and RAW linear paths, and a sample equal to the rendered byte; and the mask overlay is a bounded display-cell coverage grid the exact preview phase returns beside its frame, for a whole mask or one component, with its mode and tint as per-client view state that commits nothing. One table describes the component kinds and drives parsing, the generated per-kind geometry methods (`mask.create-radial`, `mask.add-linear`, `mask.set-radial`) and the generated controls, so registering a kind is what makes it creatable; the whole combination vocabulary is delivered, including a duplicate that copies the layers bound to the mask. A masked recipe stays proxy eligible and its proxy frame is byte for byte the exact recipe over the exact downscale; a mask feature thinner than two proxy pixels has its **field** (not its effect) supersampled 2×2 on the proxy phase only, and the frame is reported approximate with a reason that distinguishes it from a spatial layer's approximation. **Mask** is a canvas-takeover mode with a Masks panel: the mask list, the component list, the linear gradient's handle editor over the photograph, and the maskable modules' own generated sections bound to the open mask rather than reimplemented inside it, with the overlay drawn from the coverage grid the preview worker already fills and the recipe list naming each masked layer's mask in the durable processing order. A draft of a *module* action now carries a mask target, so a masked slider previews the masked layer it is about to commit exactly as a global one does. Ahead of the brush that uses them, the host path primitives are delivered — a `points` parameter kind, a stored coordinate grid of 16384 steps per unit, a deterministic decimation contract and the stroke — together with a content-addressed stroke store, so a stroke is stored once under a hash of its contents and no catalog holds embedded points; a reference the store cannot answer refuses rendering, sampling, export and planning alike by name, retaining everything, and is never read as an empty stroke. Measured, that store takes a 200-stroke history from 20.4 MB to 1.11 MB — a constant factor of about 28. Growth stays quadratic and is not claimed otherwise. The path kind, the stroke list and the `brush-paint` capture are host primitives the Corrections brush geometry shares rather than duplicates. The range selections' mathematics is frozen by the [range study](design/range-study.md) against an independent `f64` reference, with the scenes it cannot separate stated and measured. **Verified on the M4 Mac** by the `mask-linear` smoke scenario — two launches over one catalog capturing Mask mode, a gradient drawn in one sweep, its release, its commit, a masked Exposure gesture, the same layer edited again from JSON by the mask's name, the coverage overlay on and off, an undo and the reopen, with each frame checked against the photograph's own measured change: the patch outside the gradient reads the same to four decimal places through all eleven frames while the patch inside tracks every edit. `mask_geometry_survival.rs` proves by rendered comparison that a crop, a straighten, a quarter-turn and a reflection leave the mask on the same content pixels, and that a reopened catalog returns the masks, components, bound layers and history with the identities it wrote. An end-to-end masked-drag latency figure is now takeable (`editor-latency --mask`) and recorded in [performance](specs/performance.md); it is provisional, taken on a host under five times the load the spec allows for quoting one, and is not a baseline. Phase B is verified on the M4 Mac by the `mask-combine` smoke scenario: one mask of four radial components — add, subtract, intersect and a second add — built on the canvas, with the composed coverage read off the `mask-on-black` overlay at four fixed points after every commit, each component's own contribution read the same way by pointing at its row, a reorder the host refuses captured with its reason, the reorder that moves the second add above the subtraction and takes back what it restored, a Presence drag through the finished mask and an undo. `masked_spatial.rs` proves masked Presence exact on the byte and RAW linear paths. The masked-drag latency figure is no longer provisional: retaken quiesced over sixteen runs, a mask costs a person's hand nothing measurable — every median lands between 16.8 and 17.9 ms whether masked or not, a difference smaller than the spread between two runs of one condition — and the masked Presence slider figure the plan listed as outstanding is taken. Masked spatial cost is recorded for one to four layers and for a mask of up to 32 components on 24 and 60 MP in [performance](specs/performance.md); a whole-frame mask costs about 12% at one layer on 24 MP and a small one is cheaper than no mask at all, because the tiles it cannot reach are copied rather than processed. Inside a radial is selected, which is the other way round from Lightroom, with the component's own Invert as the exact complement. **Not built:** the canvas pick that adds a colour-range swatch, the overlay for a mask that reads pixels (an open proposal), and the colour-constrained brush |
