How a colour range works
Select by colour

Pick a colour and everything that colour is selected, wherever it is in the photo. Brightness doesn’t count, so a lit wall and its shadow are selected together.

Got it ↵

A modal sheet for one kind: 720 pt wide, centred over a scrim. The eyedropper stays armed behind it. Got it, Return, Escape or ✕ close it, and Escape closes the sheet before it reaches the eyedropper. A press on the scrim does nothing. The drawings are composed from theme tokens and the twelve patches of the range fixture, never shipped as images. Every sentence comes from the host’s kind table and the picker’s steps, and the keys come from the keymap, so a kind registered later brings its own sheet.
