# Detail numerical study

Status: independent numerical reference frozen for the implementation prototype; **quality qualification is incomplete**. The broad correlated-chroma, proposed proxy p99 and literal noisy-ramp neighbour-jump gates fail. Full-pipeline probes and inspection of the four supplied photographs add terminal-chroma and fine-detail preservation concerns. These failures are not passing evidence.

The independent `f64` equations live in `crates/luxforge-reference/src/detail.rs`. This crate imports no production crate. `tests/studies/detail.rs` generates and reloads the 40 deterministic specifications and complete reference outputs in `fixtures/detail/cases.json`, and recomputes all 116 rows of `measurements.json`. The production implementation is checked against those outputs rather than used to generate them. The measurement report retains five failing rows: the two rejected three-level chroma controls and the four-level sigma-16, proxy p99 and noisy-ramp gates.

## Frozen equations and mappings

The eight fields are sharpening 0–150 (gain `/100`), radius 0.5–3.0 px (Gaussian sigma, default 1), sharpen-detail 0–100 (default 25), sharpen-masking 0–100 (default 0), luminance 0–100 (default 0), luminance-detail 0–100 (default 50), colour 0–100 (default 0) and colour-detail 0–100 (default 50). All three strengths default to zero. Ancillary fields remain stored and editable at zero strength.

Each unit converts signed linear sRGB to the project's Oklab, retaining extended values. Every separable smoothing pass uses `center + Σ weight * (sample − center)`, excluding the center tap. B3 taps occur in order −2, −1, +1, +2. Gaussian taps occur in ascending coordinate order excluding zero. Constants are therefore exact. A zero accumulated Lab delta returns the original RGB without conversion back. Otherwise, when both reconstructed opponent channels have absolute value at most `1e-6`, all channels equal `L³`; other values use the published Oklab inverse. No intermediate RAW clipping or gamut clamp occurs.

Noise reduction uses three luminance and **four chroma** undecimated B3 levels with spacings 1, 2, 4 and 8. The fourth level affects chroma only. Band noise factors are 0.8914, 0.1992, 0.0860 and 0.0417. The fixed moderate settings are luminance 40, colour 40, sharpening 50 and radius 1, with ancillary defaults. Thresholds are `T_L = 0.10*(luminance/100)^1.5*k` and `T_C = 0.10*(colour/100)^1.5*k`. This threshold tuning was needed because the initial 0.05/0.04 baseline reduced the strongest dark-patch noise by only 8.3%. The frozen values keep the selected formulation and pass the flat-patch threshold without increasing scale count beyond the contingency.

Protection uses a direct 3×3 box mean of the band's squared lightness, or the chroma vector's squared magnitude. `π = energy/(energy+(3T)²)` and `T_eff = T*(1−detail/100*π)`. Non-negative garrote applies `max(0,1−T_eff²/magnitude²)` to the scalar or whole chroma vector. Zero magnitude contributes zero delta. The sum of shrinkage deltas is added to the original Lab rather than reconstructed from separately rounded bands.

Sharpening smooths lightness with a sampled normalized Gaussian of sigma radius and support `ceil(3*radius)`. The residual is `L−blur`; coring is `r*r²/(r²+θ²)`, with `θ=0.01*(1−sharpen-detail/100)²`. The guide is a Gaussian of sigma 1, with central differences in both axes. Masking is 1 at zero and otherwise `E²/(E²+(sharpen-masking/100*0.05)²)`. Gain is sharpening/100. A 3×3 lightness min/max limits the proposed value: inside the range it is unchanged; above/below it uses `hi + δ*tanh((proposed−hi)/δ)` or `lo + δ*tanh((proposed−lo)/δ)`, with `δ=0.04*(hi−lo)`. A zero range returns the input lightness.

## Support, sampled kernels and resources

True image edges clamp; tile, region and crop cuts require the full support. Exact denoise support is 31 px with four chroma levels (15 px for three). Exact sharpening support is `max(ceil(3*radius),4)`, at most 9 px. The full-resolution denoise/sharpen caps are 32/16 px, and the full-resolution operation cap is 48 px.

At a sampled scale `(sx,sy)`, each denoise level uses a Gaussian per axis with `sigma=h*s`, radius `max(1,ceil(3*sigma))`, normalized in f64 and then cast in production. No level is floored or skipped; underflowing noncentral weights yields identity. Exact scale 1 uses B3. Thresholds stay fixed. Protection stays 3×3. Sharpen blur sigma is radius times scale; guide sigma is scale and central-difference/limiter radii remain at least one pixel. Denoise halo is the larger per-axis sum of the level radii plus one; sharpen halo is the larger of blur radius and guide radius plus one. Four-level sampled denoise can reach **46 px near scale 1**, so its sampled cap is 46 and the evaluated operation cap is 62; the full-resolution cap is not a proxy clamp.

Scratch is checked before allocation: denoise `13*4*w*h` bytes (Lab 3, coarse 3, next 3, separable temporary 1, delta 3), sharpen `6*4*w*h` bytes (Lab 3, temporary 1, blur 1, guide 1). The direct 3×3 energy calculation uses scalar accumulators. Scratch is the maximum of the units, not the sum. No full-frame Lab image is required in production. Units declare support once from their compile context; evaluating a cut cannot change it. The host passes its cancellation token to each unit; Detail checks every wavelet level and every row of each separable, conversion and reconstruction pass. A cancelled unit returns no tile output, and the host releases its reservation.

The production tolerance is `1e-5 + 1e-5*|reference|` in linear light and at most one terminal output code at a quantization boundary. The rounded f32 Oklab round trip alone can differ by 2.6e-6 in gamut and 1.1e-5 for extended values, so 1e-6 would be below the conversion floor. Constants, copy-if-unchanged, point/full, region/full and serial/pool equality are exact requirements.

## Gate dispositions

Measurements are synthetic, not photographic guarantees. The flat-patch probes use 256×256 levels 0.01, 0.05, 0.18 and 0.45, both prescribed Poisson–Gaussian pairs, SplitMix64 plus Box–Muller, JPEG quantization or f32 RAW input, and central 192×192 green/Rec.709 luma statistics. Full per-configuration reductions and mean drifts remain in the measurement file, including any failing rows.

The 5° slanted edge uses 4×4 integration of optical Gaussian sigma 0.5 and 0.8, linear 0.10→0.50 and 0.10→0.78 edges, sigma 0.0015 noise, quarter-pixel ESF bins, a central-difference LSF, Hann window and direct Fourier evaluation of the real signal. MTF50 uses interpolation at the 0.5 crossing. Moderate sharpening improves MTF50 by 20.5–30.6%; overshoot/undershoot stays below 2.23% of contrast, passing the 10%/5% gates.

The correlated-chroma probe uses Gaussian-smoothed normal fields at sigma 8 and 16, normalized to RMS 0.003 in Oklab a with b=a/2, on L=0.6. Three levels fail both cases. The specified fourth chroma level raises sigma-8 reduction to 26.0%, passing 25%; sigma-16 reaches only **14.1%, failing 25%**. The four-scale coarse residual retains this broad structure even when all included bands are removed. The reference records the failure; no fifth scale has been accepted or implemented.

The 384×257 proxy probe is a noisy multiaxis grating with noninteger sampled axis scales at reductions 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8. Errors use unrounded encoded-code units. The selected Gaussian rule stays within the proposed RMS 1.2-code bound, but its p99 at 2× is **4.13 codes**, exceeding the proposed 3. The proposal is therefore rejected by this probe, not silently relaxed to a passing gate. Clamp and skip controls, with their full figures, are retained in the same file; the selected rule has lower RMS at every tested scale. This establishes a measured approximation, not an exact moving preview.

The dark-ramp probe holds input codes 8–40 with 1.5-code Gaussian noise. After denoise it compares 8-bit, 16-bit and float hand-offs, then exposure +2 EV and the independent Basic Shadows +100 curve. Actual adjacent output-code jumps reach 15 codes with 8-bit and **16 codes with either 16-bit or float**, failing the literal one-code gate. Stochastic noise survives the garrote, so this metric cannot isolate quantization contours. Row-averaged neighbouring columns reach 0.625 codes with 8-bit, 0.578 with 16-bit and 0.516 with float; these are supplementary diagnostics, not substitutes for the failed gate. A revised banding-isolation metric has not been accepted. The production gate must not claim success based on these averages.

The three known failing gate tests carry explicit ignored reasons and can be run individually with `--ignored`; they retain the original assertions and fail when explicitly run. Their skips are incomplete qualification. Passing default reference tests proves fixture consistency, the independent flat-patch/edge/RMS properties and the independent sigma-8 chroma gate. It does not qualify the terminal production chroma result below.

## Supplied photographic inspection

The four authentic sources in `fixtures/detail/corpus.json` were processed through `EditorService::render_current`, with default RAW development/as-shot white balance and no Basic/Presence changes. Every full frame's fingerprint matches the source's listed SHA256, and the originals' hashes match again after rendering. Baseline has no Detail; moderate is Luminance 40, Colour 40, Amount 50, Radius 1 with ancillary defaults. Noise stress is Luminance/Colour 100, both noise-detail fields 0 and Amount 0. Sharpen stress is Amount 150, Radius 3, Detail 100, Masking 0 and both noise strengths 0. All eight fields are supplied per processed configuration, so one configuration cannot inherit another's strengths.

All 13 listed 256×256 crops were inspected pixel for pixel in baseline/moderate/noise-stress/sharpen-stress comparisons. Final-size views use the shared linear-light reducer over each exact final raster, at 1024 px on the longest side; they use no moving proxy approximation. Current image evidence and correlated source/crop manifests are under `artifacts/detail-quality-20260930/`, with the additional Nikon face/hair crop under `artifacts/detail-quality-nikon-face-20260930/`. The evidence utilities live in `fixtures/detail/`; private RAW pixels and derived image evidence are not added to the fixture repository.

These are visual observations from the supplied captures, not controlled ISO/noise qualification. “Caution” means the observed fine-detail loss needs a product/quality disposition. “Fail at stress” describes visible damage in that deliberate maximum-strength configuration; it is not a passing moderate-quality claim.

Known ISO/exposure classes, paired registered clean/noisy captures, and the full skin/hair/fabric/sky/coloured-edge/repeating-pattern matrix remain unavailable. The synthetic failures, the observed moderate fine-detail attenuation and these gaps prevent a quality-complete claim; unavailable cells are not marked passed.
